# Rule: Editorial Brutalism & Technical Neo-Brutalism Guidelines

## 1. Zero AI-Glow Trope Policy
- NEVER use generic soft dark-mode radial glows, diffuse purple/cyan gradients, or fuzzy floating blurred orbs.
- Use stark, confident solid contrast: Crisp paper-white backgrounds (`#fafaf7`, `#ffffff`) or deep obsidian black (`#0a0a0a`), paired with bold solid botanical green (`#008a3d`, `#16a34a`) color blocking.

## 2. Editorial Architectural Typography
- Main Display: Heavy, bold sans-serif with tight tracking (`tracking-tighter`, `font-black`, `leading-none`) inspired by high-fashion editorial publications (e.g. Bottega Veneta).
- Monospace Data Layer: Use `Space Mono` or `JetBrains Mono` for metadata, coordinates, bracketed technical specs (`[REF // 2025]`, `[BOTANICAL SEED MATRIX]`), and phonetic pronunciations (`[VER'DA:VI:A]`).
- Kinetic & Orbital Text: Circular curved text badges and linear mechanical tape marquees.

## 3. High-Contrast Organic & Geometric Layouts
- High-contrast color blocking using solid organic silhouette vectors and geometric asymmetric grids.
- Hard crisp borders (`border-black` or `border-neutral-900`) and tactile mechanical cards.
- Bold geometric markers: Solid downward triangles (`▼`), heavy black bars, index brackets (`[01]`), and clean photo cutouts overlapping bold typography.
